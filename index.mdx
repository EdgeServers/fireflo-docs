FireFlo sits between applications that want to send SMS and the carriers that actually deliver it. It
speaks HTTP to your applications and SMPP to your vendors, in both directions, and it keeps the
routing, the pricing and the record of every message on your own infrastructure.

It is three things that ship together:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The gateway" icon="server">
    A Java/Quarkus service. Accepts SMPP binds and HTTP submissions, routes, rates and records every
    message, and dials out to your vendors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The control panel" icon="sliders">
    Your operators' screens. Vendors, listeners, routing, customers, tariffs, statements and live
    health — configuration and diagnosis in one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The customer portal" icon="users">
    Your customers' own half of that application: their messages, their statement, their sender-ID
    registrations, credential rotation and an API playground.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
