Statuses FireFlo returns on `submit_sm_resp` beyond the standard SMPP set. **This is the whole list.**

| Code | Name | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `0xC3` | `ESME_RMISSINGOPTPARAM` | A TLV listed in the listener's `mandatory.tlvs.submit` held no value after credential defaults and any whitelist stamp were applied. |
| `0x402` | `STATUS_INSUFFICIENT_CREDIT` | The account has insufficient credit — prepaid balance exhausted, or a postpaid credit limit reached. |
| `0x403` | `STATUS_NO_UNITS` | From 0.16.0. A login of a [units account](/platform/billing/units-accounts) has no units of its product left. Nothing was charged. The default of `conf.status.noUnits`, settable per listener. |
| `0x40F` | `STATUS_INVALID_PRODUCT` | Refused at **bind**, not at submit: the resolved product is blank, or names no enabled row in `app_product`. Only when `conf.product.required` is on. |
| `0x418` | `STATUS_NO_RATE` | The message matched no sell-side rate rule. Only when `smsg.rating.unrated.refuse` is on; otherwise it is accepted and charged nothing. |
| `0x14` | `ESME_RMSGQFUL` | The router queue or this listener's inbound queue is at its limit (only when `smsg.queue.capacity` or `inQueue.capacity` is set; both default to unbounded). From 0.17.x, also too many of the account's messages already waiting for their credit (`creditQueueFull`: 10,000 per account, 100,000 per gateway). Nothing was charged. |
| `0x58` | `ESME_RTHROTTLED` | Over a messages-per-second ceiling, the login's or the account's. From 0.17.x, also a funded account whose credit was not confirmed within the wait limit (`creditPending`, see [Prepaid credit](/platform/billing/prepaid-credit#waiting-for-credit)). Nothing was charged. The credit case is the default of `conf.status.creditPending`, settable per listener; before 0.17.x it answered `0x14`. |

## What each is deliberately not

**`0x402` is not `ESME_RTHROTTLED`.** Throttled tells a client to back off and retry, which is exactly
wrong for a condition only a top-up or an invoice can clear. A client that retries a credit refusal
burns its own capacity achieving nothing.

**`0x58` for credit not confirmed in time is the opposite case.** The account has money; its credit
could not be confirmed within `smsg.balance.park.seconds`, usually because the database was slow or not
answering. A resend a moment later goes through, which is what a client does on `0x58`.

<Note>
Prepaid and postpaid refuse **identically**. `0x402` over SMPP, `402` with `insufficientCredit` over
HTTP, whichever mode the account is in. A customer's integration does not need to know which they are
on.
</Note>

**`0x403` is not `0x402`.** A units account running out of its own units is a different condition from
its provider running out of money, and only whoever gives it units can clear it. For a units login,
`0x402` means its **parent's** credit ran out; the unit it took is given back.

**`0x14` and `0x58` are well-behaved backpressure signals.** An ESME that respects them backs off and
resends. They are the refusals here that a client *should* retry: a full queue, a full credit-waiting
queue, a rate ceiling and credit not confirmed in time all clear by themselves.

**`0x40F` carries one code and two distinct WARN lines** — blank product versus unknown name. The
client only ever sees the number, so the log is where those are told apart.

## Vendor-side classification

Codes a **vendor** returns are classified by `status.retry.worker` and `status.retry.router` — see
[Vendor settings](/reference/smpp/client).

`ESME_RSUBMITFAIL` (`0x45`, decimal 69) is treated as permanent. It is genuinely ambiguous across
SMSCs — some mean transient overload — so it is a per-vendor opt-back-in rather than a global
reclassification.

## Where a refusal is recorded

| Ingress | Refusal | Recorded as |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SMPP | `submit_sm_resp` with the code | A `cdr_rejected` row |
| REST | `402`, `503` or `429` | A `cdr_rejected` row with a reason |

Nothing refused at ingress is charged. The REST path charges before it publishes, so a queue-full
refusal there refunds; the SMPP path refuses before accepting, so there is nothing to refund.

See [Refusals](/platform/troubleshooting/refusals) for reading them back.
